About StayEase

Our Aim

To provide a simple online platform where customers can search hotels, view room information, compare prices and reserve rooms.

Our Features

  • Room search and availability
  • Customer registration and login
  • Online booking simulation
  • Booking history and cancellation
  • Administrator room management
  • Responsive website design

FAQs and Travel Guide

How can I book a room?

Search your dates, select an available room and log in to confirm your booking.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. This demo allows customer cancellation at least 3 days before check-in.

Is real payment collected?

No. This is a student project demonstration and does not process payments.

What should I know before travelling?

Confirm hotel check-in times, carry valid identification and check local transport options.
